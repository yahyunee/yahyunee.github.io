About Me

I'm an Integrated M.S. and Ph.D. student in Brain and Cognitive Sciences at Seoul National University (Connectome Lab, advised by Prof. Jiook Cha), passionate about understanding the brain through the lens of artificial intelligence and computational neuroscience. My research focuses on EEG analysis, neuroimaging, and developing foundation models for brain signals.

I'm currently a Visiting Student Research Collaborator at Princeton University, working in Uri Hasson's Lab (Department of Psychology).

With a background in Life Science and Computer Science Engineering from POSTECH, I bridge biological understanding with computational methods to tackle challenging problems in neuroscience and psychiatry.
