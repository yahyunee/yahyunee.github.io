About Me

I'm a PhD student in Brain and Cognitive Sciences at Seoul National University, passionate about understanding the brain through the lens of artificial intelligence and computational neuroscience. My research focuses on EEG analysis, neuroimaging, and developing foundation models for brain signals.

With a background in Life Science and Computer Science Engineering from POSTECH, I bridge biological understanding with computational methods to tackle challenging problems in neuroscience and psychiatry.
